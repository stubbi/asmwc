# Code of conduct

Everyone is welcome to participate. Treat other people with respect, give technical feedback about the work rather than the person, and assume that contributors may have different backgrounds or levels of experience.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexualized conduct, deliberate disruption, and publishing someone else's private information are not acceptable in this project's issues, pull requests, or other project spaces. Do not continue unwanted contact after someone asks you to stop.

The maintainer may edit or remove content, close discussions, limit participation, or block an account when necessary to protect participants and keep the project useful. Responses should be proportionate to the behavior and its impact.

Report concerns to [@stubbi](https://github.com/stubbi) using a private contact method listed on the maintainer's profile. If no private method is available, open an issue asking for a private contact channel without including sensitive details. Do not post a public account of another person's private information or a sensitive incident. The maintainer will consider reports in context; this document does not promise a particular response time or outcome.
